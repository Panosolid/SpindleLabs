- Move: a Move button in Modify (and the M key) opens a panel to move and turn bodies. Click the bodies to move (a click on a face picks its whole body), type distances along X, Y and Z and angles about each axis, and the bodies move on screen as you type; they turn about the middle of the picked bodies unless you type a pivot. OK adds the Move to the timeline as one undo step.
- A Move in the timeline can be edited with Edit feature (or a double-click), keeping the pivot it was made with.
- A number that isn't one, or a move out past the edge of model space, is refused with a reason, and nothing changes.
- On a phone the view stays clear while you pick the bodies, and the panel then takes well under half of the view.
