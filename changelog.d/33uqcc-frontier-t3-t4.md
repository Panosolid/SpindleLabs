- Testing (T3, frontier testing): issues #352 and #355 were fixed in the step-32 batch 2, so the same-answers test no longer lets them through as known issues. The two pairs that showed them are kept as a test that they stay fixed. With the later step-32 hunt batches merged, the test found two new kinds of difference when a pair is mirrored or turned exactly: which of two tied corners places their shared point (#377), and which refusal is given when a pair has several faults (#378). Both are filed as design questions for the booleans and counted as known until decided.
- Testing (T4 (3/3), frontier testing): the devious sketch tests shrink what they find too. A failing random part is cut down (steps dropped, arcs straightened, numbers simplified) to the smallest part that still fails the same way, and saved and replayed like the others. With a deliberate error planted in the volume of bodies with more than five faces, 164 of 300 random parts failed and the shrinker handed back a unit square extruded 1 mm. Shrinking the boolean judge's disagreements waits for the booleans themselves (step 35).
- Testing (T4 (2/3), frontier testing): the facts-table runs now shrink what they find. When the exact-moves run or the lineage run fails, it shrinks the failing pair (up to three kinds of failure) with its own check, prints the small version and saves it in `kernel/tests/regressions/shrunk/`, and a replay test keeps every saved pair passing once fixed. Tried on deliberate kernel breaks: a 1 km pair came back as two small triangular prisms keeping only the one near miss that matters, and a bug that ignored z came back as two boxes far out along z. Issue #355's real failing pair, 100 km out, shrinks to two small prisms near the origin that still show it.
- Testing (T4 (1/3), frontier testing): a shrinker for failing cases (`kernel/tests/support/reduce.rs`). Given a case that fails a check, it tries simpler versions one at a time (drop steps of a chain, corners of a prism, holes and corners of a profile; straighten arcs; move towards the origin; simpler numbers: 0, coarser grids, fewer bits, halves) and keeps one only if it still fails the same way, numbers aside, until nothing simpler does. Every step it keeps makes the case strictly smaller, so it always ends. Tested on planted failures with their minimal cases worked out by hand: a chain to its two steps that matter, a panic to the number 128, two untidy boxes 1 km out to two small triangular prisms whose edges cross at (1, 1, 1), and a refused profile with arcs and three holes to two triangles. It never trades one failure for another. Next it gets wired into the random tests, so a failure comes with its smallest version.
- Testing (T3 (2/2), frontier testing): lineage. Every shared vertex of the facts table is now checked in exact arithmetic (`kernel/tests/lineage.rs`, referees in `kernel/tests/support/lineage.rs`): it is on exactly the vertices, edges and faces of both bodies that it reaches (none missing, none out of reach), it is traced to the facts that made it (every input vertex, edge crossing and edge–face crossing merged exactly once, placed by its best source, its sources' own entities and the edges and faces at its vertices all in its list), and every place where the two bodies touch, worked out from the bodies alone, has a shared vertex on both. The pairs are the 30c generator's and its touching pairs pushed along one axis, each also with B's tolerances loosened and with target and tool swapped, plus a new family the generator lacked: a cube stood on one corner with that corner halfway up another cube's edge, pushed by 0.9 and 1.1 of the summed tolerance every way. 2,134 tables and 38,000 shared vertices in 15 s on all cores, clean. Hand-worked cases (two cubes corner to corner: one shared vertex on 14 entities, listed; a box standing on a face; a corner on an edge), 0.9×/1.1× of a vertex's tolerance above a face, and twelve kinds of wrong entry each caught with its own words. Seven deliberate kernel breaks are caught alone (the tolerance not grown to reach, a face's reach halved, placed by the worst source, the re-check's edge reach short, the crossing's rounding left out, the re-check joining nothing, the re-check finding no edges); six more break a path the kernel backs up with a second one, and each is caught once its backup is broken too. The other half checks a result's faces: each comes from one input face (in its plane, inside it, facing the same way out, or the other way for a tool face kept by a subtract) and no input face gives more area than it has; booleans hand it their history from 35b. Checked on a box cut by hand (A − B and A ∩ B, areas worked out) and seven kinds of wrong origin. The facts-table crash that #329 fixed is now off the known list in the T3 (1/2) checks.
- Testing (T3 (1/2), frontier testing): same answers under exact moves. A quarter turn, a mirror, a scale by a power of two or a move on the grid is exact in doubles (`kernel/tests/support/metamorphic.rs` maps a body bit for bit, turning its loops round under a mirror). So the facts table of a moved pair must be the table of the pair moved back: the same facts between the same ids, the same shared vertices, and every number within 8 rounding steps (the worst seen is 2.6). The same holds with target and tool swapped, with every face's loop started one coedge later, and with B's tolerances loosened to 3·LINEAR so the two bodies differ. The pairs are the 30c generator's, plus its touching pairs pushed along one axis by each band offset: 181,000 tables a seed, about 25 s; 8 seeds (1.45 million tables) are clean apart from two known issues. It re-found the facts-table crash that #329 fixed. It found issue #352 (a crossing near two edges of a face is put on whichever edge the face's loop lists first; already fixed in the step-32 batch 2) and issue #355 (the same for an edge lying in the face's plane; not fixed yet, and still there on batch 3). Each known issue has a check that fails loudly once it is fixed. General turns, which aren't exact, are compared within LINEAR and their differences listed as findings: 516 tables, 6 findings, all coplanar facts of faces that only a turned box makes near. Extrudes: a profile and distance scaled by 2^k give the extrude scaled by 2^k bit for bit (4 profiles, 5 scales), and a frame given each of the 24 quarter turns gives the same local body bit for bit and the turned corners. Checked by hand-worked maps (a quarter turn of (1, 2, 3) is (−2, 1, 3); 24 turns and 24 mirrors; exact and inexact moves), a mapped box's volume and area, a mirror without its loops turned round refused by validation, and nine kinds of wrong fact each caught by the comparison. Six of seven deliberate kernel breaks are caught: the in-face test on world x and y, corner distances without z, near boxes without z, a crossing's rounding bound from x alone, A's tolerance counted twice for corners, and a corner's plane side at LINEAR instead of its own tolerance. The seventh, A's tolerance counted twice for edge crossings, needs edges crossing skew within the band with different tolerances, which the generator's pairs don't make (their parallel faces are rightly refused first); the near-miss checks (T2) catch it.
