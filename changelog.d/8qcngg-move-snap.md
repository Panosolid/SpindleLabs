- Move has a Snap button: click a face or edge of the bodies you are moving, then a face or edge of another body, and it lands there. A flat face sits flat on the other face, centred, turning the part over if it has to; a straight edge lies along the other edge; a round edge sits centred on the other round edge, like a lid on a can. The numbers fill in so you can adjust them before OK.
- On a phone, Snap puts the Properties sheet away while you tap the view and brings it back with the numbers and OK.
- While Snap is picking, the drag arrows and rings are hidden so a tap always picks.
