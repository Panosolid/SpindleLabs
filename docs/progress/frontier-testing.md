# Frontier testing

The testing workstream's progress file: harnesses that keep the boolean and curved-surface testing at the level of the best kernels and testing cultures, built while usage is plentiful so they keep catching bugs cheaply later. Ben's asks (2026-10-26): build T1–T6 before Combine (35f), then T7, a cold review proving all six work; put the rest in the roadmap at their places; run these threads one at a time, never two at once.

Where each idea comes from (checked against the sources on 2026-10-26): swarm testing, Groce et al., ISSTA 2012 (42% more distinct compiler crashes than the all-on default); "sometimes" assertions, Antithesis; BUGGIFY, FoundationDB; near-miss inputs, Kettner et al., "Classroom examples of robustness problems in geometric computations"; affine-equivalent inputs, Spatter (34 bugs in spatial databases, most in the geometry library); test-case reduction, C-Reduce (PLDI 2012); Manifold's FuzzTest of random union/difference chains; Trettner et al. 2021's milling endurance test (Cork failed after 17 cuts); fault injection, SQLite's anomaly tests; known-failure marks that report a surprise fix, OpenCascade's DRAW `TODO` (BAD, IMPROVEMENT).

- [ ] T2. Near-miss generator for the facts table (Kettner's method), with an exact referee on the stored doubles (`kernel/tests/support/exact.rs`: num-rational fractions, distances compared squared).
  - [x] T2a "T2 (1/2)". The point decisions: `kernel/tests/near_miss.rs`. A 10 mm box A (square or turned, at the origin, 1 km and 90 km out; its faces as made or rewritten with reversed normals and sense false; tolerances LINEAR or grown (corners 3·LINEAR, edges 2·LINEAR; until #329 it was 2 and 3, which #348 made illegal)), a 3 mm box B turned so one corner points at A's corner, edge middle or face, that corner put the threshold away plus −16..16 steps of one ulp at that distance from the origin. Every vertex–vertex, vertex–edge and vertex–face answer of `table(A, B)` on both bodies is checked exactly: the kernel may differ only within 8 ulps of the largest coordinate involved, and never exactly at the threshold (a "low" form lands exactly on it). 9,504 tables, 3.9 million facts, 13 s. Every group must straddle its threshold. Found no kernel bug, on main and on #328+#340 and #329: the kernel differed from exact 70 times, all within 0.3 ulps. Eight deliberate breaks each caught (the summed tolerance 1e-9 high, an edge's whole line instead of the edge, the side test 1e-7 loose, `<=` made `<` (only the exact-threshold form sees it), face sense ignored, `>` made `>=` in the side test, an edge's tolerance ignored on either body). FISSION_NEAR_MISS_SPINS=n adds seeded turns: seeds 1 and 2 with 20 each (157 million facts, 6 min each in release) were clean too, the kernel never more than 0.5 ulps from exact.
  - [x] T2b "T2 (2/2)". The crossing decisions, same referee (`exact.rs` gains the exact distance between two segments, a line's crossing of a plane as a fraction point, and point-in-convex-face by triple products). B's 3 mm edge is put across each decision of `table(A, B)`: passing A's edge x = y = 10 at 60°, 1e-3 and 1e-6 rad; passing over that edge's end (the lines cross, the segments don't); pointing away from it, its end beside it; piercing A's top face beside its edge, inside the face or just beyond it, going down or up. Each sits the summed edge tolerance plus −16..16 ulp steps out (and 0, 0.5, 0.9, 1.1 and 2 times it), at the origin, 1 km and 90 km out, square and turned, A's faces either way round and its tolerances as made or loose; B's two faces through the edge are turned 30° (not 45°) from A's, or a nearly parallel pair would bring a face of each within 1e-6 rad, which the table rightly refuses. Checked exactly on every table: every edge–edge fact (a crossing within the summed edge tolerances; a shared corner or overlap within the loosest reach of the two), every edge–face crossing (its point within its own `err` of the plane and of the line; Inside or Outside never within the summed tolerance of a face edge, and the right one when clear of them; on an edge or corner only within reach of it), and no missing crossing (ends strictly either side and crossing inside the face). 15,336 tables, 183,000 exact checks, about 60 s (run on all cores). The kernel differed from exact 100 times, all within 0.65 ulps; clean on main, #328+#340 and #329. Deliberate breaks: the first round caught 6 of 9 and the misses showed gaps in the generator (no edge going up through a face, so dropping crossings that start below went unseen; no case deep inside the band, so face edges never holding a crossing went unseen; no edge passing beyond another's end, so an unclamped parameter went unseen); after widening, 9 of 9 (summed edge tolerance 1e-9 high or low, only B's tolerance, the parameter unclamped, the crossing's `err` 0, its point 1e-12 off, face edges never holding, `inside_face` inverted, crossings starting below dropped). A tenth, dropping the re-clamp in `closest_on_segments`, can only matter for an edge looser than its corner (otherwise the corner touches first and the pair is `AtCorner`), so a case for that was added, and it found a kernel bug, issue #348: edges looser than their corners (B's at 3e-5, its corners at 1e-5, a corner 2e-5 beside A's edge) give InvalidResult on main (the merge never settles) and with #328 crossings placed Inside/Outside within a face edge's reach; fixed by #329 (the facts table's input check refuses an edge looser than its corners; the `validate` rule follows in the step 32 review's batch 3): the test `edges_looser_than_their_corners_are_refused` checks all 568 tables of that family are refused, naming the tool's edge and corner. Long runs: FISSION_NEAR_MISS_SPINS=12 with seeds 1 and 2 (24 turns each, 2.4 and 2.6 million exact checks, about 16 min each in release): clean, the kernel never more than 1.7 ulps from exact. Two planes meeting (`meet::planes`) is checked by 32b, which uses it first (the facts table doesn't). PR: #342. - [ ] T3. Same-answer checks under exact moves, and lineage
  - [ ] T3a. `kernel/tests/support/metamorphic.rs`: maps that are exact in doubles (quarter turns about the axes, mirrors, axis swaps, moves by powers of two on the grid, scales by powers of two) applied to the inputs of every operation the harnesses run (primitives, extrude, placed extrude, the facts table, from 35c the booleans): the result's topology must be identical, faces map to faces, volume and area scale by the exact factor, and swapping A and B gives the same facts. Any map that isn't exact (a general turn) is compared within tolerance only, and a topology change there is a finding to look at, not a failure.
  - [ ] T3b. A lineage checker: every face of a result comes from exactly one input face (the same plane, the same outward side) and every input face's area is accounted for (kept, split or removed). For the facts table first (each fact names the entities it came from); for booleans it reads 35's history map (B0 decision 6), so its boolean half lands with 35c. Later it checks names through timeline replays (40).
- [ ] T4. An automatic shrinker for failing parts (C-Reduce style)
  - [ ] T4a. `kernel/tests/support/reduce.rs`: given a failing case (an operation chain, a part, a boolean pair) and the check it fails, apply passes until none helps: drop an operation, a body, a hole, a corner, an arc (made a line); snap a coordinate to a coarser grid (2^k), round a number to fewer bits, move the part towards the origin; keep a change only if the case still fails the same way (same check, same message kind). Hand tests: planted failing cases that must shrink to a known minimal case.
  - [ ] T4b. Wired in: devious, boolean_cases, judge.py disagreements and the facts-table run write the shrunk case beside the original in their failure folders and in `tests/regressions/`.
- [ ] T5. The boolean fuzz target and an endurance chain (lands with 35c): a cargo-fuzz target `boolean` whose input is an operation tree decoded with `arbitrary` (up to 100 primitives and extrusions, exact and general moves, union/subtract/intersect), `validate` after every step, a call over 10 s counted as a hang; and a long chain of cuts (200 cuts into one block, Trettner's milling test) checked after every cut against the integer grid model (36a, `grid_model.rs`).
- [ ] T6. Fault injection: a thread-local counter in the kernel (debug builds) and FISSION_FAIL_AT=<n> make the nth sub-step (an intersection, a face split, a classification) report failure. The operation must return a clean `KernelError`, leave its inputs untouched (bit for bit), and the next call must work; in the app, a rebuild with a failing feature keeps the document valid and undoable. Run for every n until the operation completes, like SQLite's out-of-memory loop.
